"The taste of fresh seafood begins long before it arrives in the kitchen"

Behind the perfect sweetness offered at big dinners is a constant 24-hour race against time. When a catch leaves the water, its 'life clock' begins a frenetic countdown, forcing fishermen and merchants into a high-stakes relay, from misty docks to the 'miniature oceans' of the metropolis. This documentary takes you behind the scenes of that midnight odyssey, where aeration technology and the art of heat management maintain the very breath of the sea's wealth.

Our Story

Adrift with the Squid-luring lights

While the city is still sleeping, fishing boats silently navigate the dark ocean, led by deck lights and the sound of rolling waves. When seafood is taken onboard, it is immediately preserved with ice or oxygenated seawater tanks to keep it fresh on the journey back to shore.

As dawn approaches, wholesale markets begin to bustle with activity. Fresh fish is sorted, traded, and transported across the city before being served at local markets, restaurants and kitchens.

Setup at the Market

At the docks, freshness is measured in minutes. Wholesale traders with sharp eyes must be agile to move seafood immediately into the first stage of preservation. This is the first transit point where the technical sorting of species begins.

Unlike farmed fish, wild sea grouper often die immediately upon leaving the water due to sudden environmental shifts.

To preserve quality, traders use specialized ice made from seawater. This continuous cold preservation is the only way to maintain the original essence and cellular integrity of wild fish.

Ice preservation onboard

Fresh catches are immediately covered with crushed ice to maintain low temperatures, so preserving texture, flavor and quality

Night Fishing Journey

Most fishing boats leave shore late at night, when the sea is calmer and many seafood species are easier to catch close to the surface

Oxygenated seawater storage

Live seafood is transported in tanks with circulating seawater and oxygen systems to keep it fresh and alive for a longer period

Freshness Inspection

To assure quality, seafood is meticulously tested before reaching markets and restaurants based on smell, texture, color, and overall appearance

Preservation Process

The Packaging Assembly Line for High-Vitality Species

For species with vigorous vitality such as lobsters, king crabs, and mantis shrimp, the docks transform into a packaging assembly line. These creatures are placed into foam boxes or plastic tanks integrated with artificial oxygen systems to ensure they remain active for the upcoming journey.

Logistics Fleet

Specialized fleet: Aeration vs Combined cooling systems

Type 1

Battery-powered Aeration

The first type uses specialized trucks equipped with battery-powered aeration to maintain oxygen levels.

Type 2

Combined cooling systems

The second, more advanced type, integrates both professional cooling units to stabilize water temperature and high-capacity aeration systems.

Vitality

The constant splashing of water and the vigorous kicking of lobsters and mantis shrimp are maintained by professional air pumps, serving as the most intuitive evidence of freshness.

Miniature Oceans
with Precision Filtration

At the restaurant, seafood is moved into glass tanks with recirculating filtration and professional aeration. Each species has a tailored environment. Lobsters vacation at 5-10°C, while sea fish, tiger shrimp, and squid swim at 18-20°C to ensure peak quality.

❄️ Lobsters: 5-10°C 🌊 Fish & Shrimp: 18-20°C
