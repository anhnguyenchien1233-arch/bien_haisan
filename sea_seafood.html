Stress & Sweetness

Fish produce lactic acid when stressed, making their flesh taste sour. Fishermen must keep them calm to preserve their natural sweetness.

🦞

Blue Blood

Lobsters have blue blood due to a protein called hemocyanin, which carries oxygen throughout their body!

🦑

3 Hearts

Squids have three hearts. When they swim fast to escape predators, their main heart stops beating temporarily!

THE SOUND OF ENGINES
AND THE RACE TO
PRESERVE FRESHNESS

As the city lights dim, fishermen in Quang Ninh go on a 12-hour midnight journey. In the immensity of the dark, high-intensity halogen arrays pierce the shroud, acting as both navigational markers and siren calls to the deep's treasures.

The moment the nets pass the gunwale, a stringent preservation routine is initiated. To avoid degradation, the catch is never left to rot on deck; instead, it is immediately placed in oxygenated tanks or buried in ice crushed from saltwater. This is essential for maintaining a perfect 0°C-4°C environment, preserving the cellular structure and immaculate taste of the ingredients from the moment they are removed from the water

THE OCEAN'S PULSE
AT THE HEART OF
THE SHORE

If the wholesale market is a mad rush to sort, Cai Dam Market is a theater of freshness. The ocean's vitality is fully displayed in stalls lined with oxygenated basins, filled with the sound of splashing water and the strong thrashing of life.

The preservation technique employs two separate "lifelines" adapted to each species:

  • Deep-sea giants (Mackerel, tuna and grouper): The landed catch is sealed in bespoke seawater ice, which penetrates deeper than freshwater ice to preserve muscle integrity and volatile oils.

High-vitality species, such as lobster and squid, are promptly moved to oxygenated tanks. These seaside basins keep the catch at top condition

THE TECHNIQUE
OF REPRODUCING
THE DEPTH

The voyage ends in a modern restaurant with sophisticated tank systems that imitate a natural environment for fish.

“

"We customize the temperature and salinity for each individual species to maximize survival rate and preserve peak flavor profile".

”

Mr. Hung, owner of Đại Hải Thủy Restaurant, shows how the restaurant replicates natural oceanic conditions using specialized multi-compartment tanks.

The procedure is guided by data-driven precision: lobsters prefer a temperature of 5°C-10°C, whereas sea fish and squid grow at 18°C-20°C. This strict environmental control is what develops complete trust with the discriminating diner.

The journey from the seafloor to the table is more than just a matter of logistics; it's a precise art of preservation that relies on human will and technological rigor, a daily endeavor to document the sea's basic flavors.
